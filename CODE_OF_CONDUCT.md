# Code of Conduct

Photo Hub is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect differing experiences and viewpoints.
- Protect other people's privacy and confidential information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private
information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Report
conduct concerns privately to a maintainer using the contact method listed on
their GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly
representing the project.
